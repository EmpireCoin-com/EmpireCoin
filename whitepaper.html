01. Project Overview

EmpireCoin (EMPC) is a digital token project being developed on The Open Network (TON). The project aims to build a transparent token foundation that can support future community participation and ecosystem initiatives.

At the date of this document, the project is in its TON testnet stage. Testnet activity is intended for development and testing; testnet tokens are not mainnet assets and should not be represented as having established market value.

02. Vision & Principles

The long-term direction of EmpireCoin is to develop a community-oriented ecosystem around the EMPC token. Development will proceed in stages, with public information and verifiable on-chain records used wherever practical.

Core principles

  • Transparency: publish token parameters, allocation information and relevant wallet references when verified.
  • Responsible development: build and test features before presenting them as available.
  • Fixed supply design: the current token configuration is intended to have a fixed supply without additional minting.
  • Community participation: explore useful ecosystem activities and community programs over time.

Mining Pool distribution

The 5,000,000 EMPC Mining allocation is distributed to eligible active staff accounts through sequential 24-hour Mining cycles. Each cycle has a base reward of 50 EMPC and may receive verified conditional bonuses up to 100%, for a maximum of 100 EMPC per cycle. A staff member must claim a completed cycle before starting another. A completed reward remains claimable and does not expire. Distribution has no fixed calendar end date and continues until the dedicated allocation is exhausted. Rewards are distributed from the reserved pool, not minted. Eligibility, conditions and availability are subject to system rules and pool balance; no earnings or token value are guaranteed.

Wallet balances and transfers should be checked independently on the relevant TON network.

Official wallet connection guidance

EmpireCoin currently recommends Tonkeeper as its only supported and recommended wallet for staff wallet connection and TON Proof ownership verification. Staff members should use the official Tonkeeper application and verify that they are connecting the wallet they personally control. Do not connect other wallet applications to the EmpireCoin Staff Portal unless the project publishes an updated official notice. This is a project support policy; it is not a claim that every other TON wallet is unsafe.

Never share your recovery phrase, seed phrase, private key, password or verification codes with anyone, including EmpireCoin staff. EmpireCoin will never ask you to disclose them. TON Proof ownership verification is a signature challenge and is not a blockchain payment. Do not send any 0.5 TON fee unless an official fee process is published and independently verified.

07. Roadmap

The roadmap below describes broad development priorities, not guaranteed delivery dates or completed milestones.

Phase 1 — Foundation

Establish the project identity, publish initial token information and maintain the public website.

Phase 2 — Transparency

Publish verified contract details, relevant wallet references and clearer documentation as information becomes available.

Phase 3 — Community

Develop official communication channels and explore community participation initiatives.

Research and build practical ecosystem utilities, subject to technical, security and operational readiness.
